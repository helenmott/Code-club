Baddeck

Cape Breton, Nova Scotia

A gentle landing on an inland sea before the Cabot Trail.

Days 12–13 1 night Drive in 5 hr 15 incl. ferry
Getting here: Cavendish to Baddeck, taking the Wood Islands to Caribou ferry (75-minute crossing, roughly CAD 86 per car) rather than doubling back over the bridge — same journey time, the boys get a boat, and you save the bridge toll — 5 hr 15 incl. ferry.

The idea

A staging night on the shore of the Bras d’Or Lake, an inland sea in the middle of Cape Breton. Warm, calm, swimmable water and a gentle landing before the Cabot Trail.

What to do

Hover or tap any photograph for its source on Wikimedia Commons.

Alexander Graham Bell National Historic Site

Much better than it sounds. Bell spent his summers here and the museum covers his kites, hydrofoils and aircraft as well as the telephone. Hands-on sections for children.

Parks Canada

Swimming in the Bras d’Or

Sheltered and noticeably warmer than the open Atlantic — a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, and effectively an inland sea.

On the map

Kidston Island

A free ferry from the Baddeck wharf to a small island with a lighthouse and a supervised beach.
